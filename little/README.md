# 🧸 ChinnaKutty's Corner (LKG)

Games for little learners who can't read yet: everything is spoken aloud, buttons are big, and there is lots of praise.
They use `shared/little.js` (letter sounds, big choices, the "🎤 I said it!" button: no microphone, a grown-up listens).

| Project | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| [letter-sounds](letter-sounds/) | Phonics: letter sounds and vowel friends |
| [blend-it](blend-it/) | Blending 2- and 3-letter words |
| [trace-it](trace-it/) | Writing letters and numbers |
| [picture-talk](picture-talk/) | New words, listening, and telling stories about pictures |
| [number-friends](number-friends/) | Numbers 1–10 (and 20) |
| [story-time](story-time/) | Listening to stories and answering questions |

**Tips for grown-ups:** keep it short (10–15 minutes), sit together, repeat the words with him, and praise the effort, not just right answers.
**Ideas:** Rhyme & Sing, Colours & Shapes, sight words, sentence building.
