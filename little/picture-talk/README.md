# 🖼️ Picture Talk

**Teaches:** vocabulary (animals, birds, fruits, vegetables, vehicles, body, plants), listening, and speaking in sentences. **Age:** 3–5.

- Look & Say: picture, name and a simple fact, read aloud; "🎤 I said it!".
- Find It: "Where is the parrot?", tap the right picture.
- Tell Me a Story: 6 picture scenes (park, kitchen, farm, rain, beach, birthday) with 5 spoken questions each, for talking with a grown-up.
