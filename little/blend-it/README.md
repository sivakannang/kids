# 🚂 Blend It

**Teaches:** blending sounds into words: 2-letter (at, in, up…) then 3-letter CVC words (c-a-t, d-o-g…). **Age:** 4–5.

- Tap each letter to hear its sound, then press Blend!: the letters slide together and the picture appears.
- "🐢 Say it slowly" sounds out the word.
- "🎤 I read it!" for praise and a star; a banner every 5 words.
