# 📚 Story Time

**Teaches:** listening, understanding and retelling stories. **Age:** 3–6.

- Classic tales retold simply: The Thirsty Crow, The Lion and the Mouse, The Hare and the Tortoise, The Monkey and the Crocodile.
- Plus a story starring the child: "ChinnaKutty (or PeriyaKutty) and the Little Kite".
- Read aloud page by page with pictures, a moral, then 3 picture questions.
