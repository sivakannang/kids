# ✍️ Trace It

**Teaches:** writing capital letters, small letters and numbers. **Age:** 3–6.

- A big dotted letter on writing lines; he colours it in with his finger in a chosen crayon.
- When 75% of the letter is traced (and the ink stays on the letter), he gets a star and praise.
- Links from Letter Sounds and Number Friends open the right letter or number.
