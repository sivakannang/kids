# 🌱 Focus Garden

**Teaches:** focus as a skill that grows with practice, and calming down before focusing. **Age:** 5–9.

- **🌻 Grow a Flower:** pick what she's doing (reading, homework, drawing, puzzle, practice) and how long (1, 5, 10 or 15 minutes). A seed grows into a flower as time passes. The screen stays quiet: Kuyil only shows short text encouragements, with no sounds until the end. If she leaves the page, the timer waits for her. Stopping early is fine: 1+ minute still plants a 🌱 sprout.
- **🫧 Calm Breathing:** a bubble grows and shrinks: breathe in 4 seconds, hold 2, out 4, five times.
- **🏡 My Garden:** every flower she has grown, total minutes focused, and days in a row. Saved in this browser only.

Stars: 1 per 5 minutes of focus (at least 1), plus 1 for a breathing session.
