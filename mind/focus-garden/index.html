🤔 Why?

Your brain is like a muscle. Every time you practise focusing, it gets a little stronger! 💪

If your mind wanders off, that's okay. Just notice it, and gently bring it back, like calling a puppy back to you. 🐶

🌠 Imagine…

Imagine a tiny seed in warm soil. Each minute you focus, it drinks a little water and reaches up towards the sunshine.

A green shoot, two little leaves, a bud… and then, pop! A beautiful flower, grown by YOU.

🤩 Wow!

Your brain has about 86 billion tiny cells called neurons, all talking to each other.

When you practise something, the paths between them get stronger, like a path in the grass that gets clearer each time you walk on it.

💬 Talk together

  • What helps you focus? What makes it hard?
  • How did you feel when your flower bloomed?
  • What is something you want to get better at by practising?
