# ❓ Why Jar

**Teaches:** asking questions and finding answers: the habit of curiosity. **Age:** 5–9.

- Type a question (or tap an idea) and put it in the jar: a star for asking (up to 5 a day).
- "Pick today's question": guess first, find out together (Kuyil knows 10 starter answers), write what you learned.
- Answers are kept on a shelf. Typing only, no microphone. Saved in this browser only.
