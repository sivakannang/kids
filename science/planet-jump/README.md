# 🪐 Planet Jump

**Teaches:** gravity is a pull, and it is different on every world. **Age:** 5–9.

- **Pick a world:** Pluto, Moon, Mercury, Mars, Uranus, Venus, Earth, Saturn, Neptune, Jupiter or the Sun (pretend!). Each has its real surface pull compared with Earth.
- **Jump:** the same 30 cm Earth jump, simulated with real physics. On the Moon it's about 1.8 m high and 3 seconds in the air; on Pluto, nearly 5 m and 8 seconds. A ruler shows 🌍 Earth jump, 🚪 door and 🦒 giraffe heights.
- **Feels like:** a school bag, watermelon, puppy or baby elephant, and how heavy it would feel there.
- **Jump board:** compares her jumps on each world.
- **Kuyil's 7 challenges,** plus Why?, Imagine…, Wow!, and Talk together cards.

Gas giants and the Sun have no solid ground, so she stands on a "magic floating platform".
