🤔 Why?

Gravity is an invisible pull. It's what makes things fall down.

Big, heavy worlds pull hard, so you can only jump a little and things feel heavy. Small worlds like the Moon pull gently, so you float up high!

🌠 Imagine…

Close your eyes. You are standing on the Moon in a puffy white space suit. The Earth is shining in the black sky.

You bend your knees and jump… up, up, up… and float slowly down like a feather. Boing! You're bouncing like a kangaroo!

🤩 Wow!

On the Moon you could jump about 6 times higher than on Earth!

Astronauts who walked on the Moon found it easier to hop like bunnies 🐰. And on the Sun, a 3 kg school bag would feel as heavy as a big 84 kg grown-up!
