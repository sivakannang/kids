# ☀️ Around the Sun

**Teaches:** a year is one trip around the Sun (365 days); seasons come from the Earth's tilt; leap years. **Age:** 6–9.

- **Orbit view:** the Earth travels around the Sun with its axis always leaning the same way. Drag it, press Play, or jump to March, June, September, December or today. Months are marked around the path.
- **Right panel:** the date, India's season, daylight hours in Bangalore vs London, and her noon shadow in Bangalore (it vanishes on Zero Shadow Day, around late April and mid-August).
- **Kuyil's 6 challenges:** June, December, long London days, short London days, Zero Shadow Day, one full trip.

Simplified: the orbit is drawn as a circle and the dates are approximate (to within a day or two).
