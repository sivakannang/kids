🤔 Why?

Why 365 days? The Earth travels all the way around the Sun once a year. The trip takes 365 days (and a few hours).

Why seasons? The Earth is tilted, like a leaning top. When our half leans towards the Sun, days are long and warm. When it leans away, days are short and cool.

🌠 Imagine…

Imagine you are riding on the Earth, like a giant spaceship, going round and round the Sun.

Every birthday you have, your spaceship has finished one more trip! How many trips have you done so far?

🤩 Wow!

The trip takes 365 days and about 6 hours. Those 6 hours add up: 4 × 6 = 24 hours = one extra day every 4 years! That's why we have 29th February in a leap year.

Twice a year in Bangalore the Sun is right overhead at noon, and your shadow disappears: Zero Shadow Day!
