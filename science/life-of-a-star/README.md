# ⭐ Life of a Star

**Teaches:** how stars are born, live and die. **Age:** 6–10.

- Pick a Sun-like, 10× or 25× star and step through its life: nebula → … → white dwarf, or supernova → neutron star / black hole.
- Star colours and temperatures (red is coolest, blue is hottest).
- Kuyil's 5 challenges.
