# 🔭 Zoom Out!

**Teaches:** the scale of the universe, from her to everything we can see. **Age:** 5–10.

- 12 zoom levels: Kavikuyil, home, neighbourhood, Bangalore, India, Earth, Earth and Moon, Solar System, nearby stars, Milky Way, Local Group, observable universe.
- Slider, buttons, mouse wheel, or "zoom all the way out".
- India and Earth use the real map shapes.
- Kuyil's 5 challenges.
