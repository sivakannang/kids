# 🌍 Spin the Earth

**Teaches:** why we have day and night, and why a day is 24 hours. **Age:** 5–8.

- **Space view:** looking down on the Earth from above the North Pole, with the Sun on the left. She drags the Earth (or presses Play) and watches Bangalore move through sunrise, noon, sunset and midnight. London, New York and Tokyo show ☀️ or 🌙.
- **Bangalore sky:** the sky outside her home changes colour, the Sun and Moon move across it, and a clock shows the time and what she might be doing (school, play, bedtime…).
- **Counters:** full spins = days, and how many km Bangalore travelled (about 1,600 km per hour).
- **Kuyil's 7 challenges:** make it night, sunrise, noon, 8 o'clock, sunset, daytime in New York, and one full spin.
- **Learn cards:** Why?, Imagine…, Wow!, and Talk together, each read aloud with 🔊.

Simplified on purpose: no Earth tilt (seasons come in "Around the Sun"), and the clock shows Bangalore sun time.
