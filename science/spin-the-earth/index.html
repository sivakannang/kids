🤔 Why?

Why day and night? The Earth is always spinning, like a top. The side facing the Sun has day ☀️. The side facing away from the Sun has night 🌙.

Why 24 hours? One full spin takes 24 hours. Long ago, people in Egypt split the day into 12 parts and the night into 12 parts. 12 + 12 = 24 hours!

🌠 Imagine…

Close your eyes. Imagine you are a tiny astronaut, floating quietly in space. 🧑‍🚀

Below you, the big blue Earth is turning slowly, like a merry-go-round. Look! India is coming out of the dark into the golden sunshine. The Sun is rising in Bangalore, and someone is just waking up… is it you?

🤩 Wow!

The Earth spins so fast that Bangalore moves about 1,600 kilometres every hour. That's faster than an aeroplane! ✈️

We don't feel it, because everything spins together: you, your house, the air and the sea.
