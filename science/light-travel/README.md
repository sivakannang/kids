# 💡 Light Travel

**Teaches:** the speed of light, looking back in time, and the light year. **Age:** 6–10.

- Send light to the Moon (plays in real time: 1.3 s), the Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Neptune, the nearest star, the middle of the galaxy, or Andromeda.
- Compare light with a rocket, a plane and a car.
- "Looking back in time" facts, and 5 challenges.
