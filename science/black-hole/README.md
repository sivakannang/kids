# ⚫ Black Hole

**Teaches:** what a black hole is, built on escape velocity. **Age:** 7–10.

- Squeeze the Earth or the Sun (same mass, smaller size) and watch the escape speed climb until it beats light: the Earth becomes a marble-sized black hole.
- Spaghettification slider (pretend!).
- Real black holes: Sagittarius A* and M87*.
