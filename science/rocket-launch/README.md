# 🚀 Rocket Launch

**Teaches:** gravity, orbits and escape velocity (Newton's cannon). **Age:** 6–10.

- Launch a ball sideways from a mountain on the Moon, Mars, Earth or Jupiter with a chosen speed. Real gravity physics: it falls, orbits, makes an oval, or escapes.
- Shows orbit and escape speeds for each world.
- Kuyil's 6 challenges. Leads into the Black Hole game.
