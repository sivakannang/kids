# 🌙 Moon Phases

**Teaches:** the Moon has no light of its own; the Sun lights half of it; we see more or less of that half as it goes around us. One trip ≈ 29½ days ≈ a month. **Age:** 5–9.

- **Space view:** Sun on the left, Earth in the middle, and the Moon going round, always lit on its Sun side. Drag it or press Play.
- **What we see from Bangalore:** the Moon's real shape, its name (with Tamil names Amavasai and Pournami), and a 30-day strip of every phase.
- **🌙 Tonight's Moon:** jumps to the real phase for today, so she can go outside and check.
- **Kuyil's 6 challenges:** full, new, half, crescent, tonight's Moon, one full trip.
