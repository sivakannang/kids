🏡 What we see from Bangalore

🐦

🤔 Why?

The Moon doesn't make its own light. The Sun always lights up half of it, like a torch shining on a ball.

As the Moon travels around the Earth, we see more or less of that bright half. One trip takes about 29½ days, and that's where the month comes from. Even the word "month" comes from "moon"!

🌠 Imagine…

Imagine lying on the terrace at night, looking up. Each night the Moon grows a little bigger, like a slice of melon getting fatter, until it's a big round Pournami Moon.

Then, night by night, it gets thinner again, until it hides away completely on Amavasai.

🤩 Wow!

The Moon is about 3,84,400 km away. A car driving non-stop would take about 160 days to get there!

And we always see the same face of the Moon. Its other side always faces away from us.
