# 🎹 Little Piano

**Teaches:** notes (Sa Re Ga / C D E), melodies, and composing. **Age:** 4–9.

- 24 keys (two octaves), labelled Sa Re Ga or C D E; computer keys A S D F… also play.
- Piano, flute and xylophone sounds.
- Follow-the-glowing-key tunes: Sa Re Ga Ma scale, Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells (all old public-domain tunes).
- Record your own tune and play it back.
