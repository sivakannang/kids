# Common Words: 2, 3 & 4-Letter Vocabulary

A comprehensive guide to building reading skills through common word groups.

---

# Level 1: Two-Letter Words

**Focus**: Recognizing common two-letter words instantly
**Time**: 10-15 minutes daily
**Goal**: Read without hesitation

---

## Word Group 1: "is" and "am"

```
is
am
```

### Read Aloud
Say each word 3-4 times:
- "**is**" - used for "he is", "she is", "it is"
- "**am**" - used for "I am"

### Small Sentences
```
I am happy.
It is hot.
She is here.
```

### Copy Practice
```
is  _ _ _ _
am  _ _ _ _

I am happy.    _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _
```

**Time**: 20-25 minutes daily
**Goal**: Reading sentences with confidence

---

```

```

### Read Aloud
- **home** - where we live
- **that** - pointing word
- **have** - possess
- **just** - only / recently
- **very** - extremely

### Small Sentences
```

I have a book.
He just came.
```
