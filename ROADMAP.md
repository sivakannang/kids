# 🌌 The Curiosity Journey

The goal is for Kavikuyil to ask **why, what and how**, to **imagine** and picture ideas in her mind, and to learn **focus, goals and dreams**. Maths and science should feel like an adventure, not a lesson.

The journeys build on each other, from things she can touch and feel to things she can only imagine:

```
 ⏰ Time ──► 📏 Measuring ──► 🌍 Earth & Sky ──► 🚀 Space ──► 🌌 The Universe
                              (why 24 h, 365 d)   (gravity)    (stars, black holes)
                    🧠 Growing Me runs alongside: focus · goals · dreams · imagination
```

## How every game is built (the "Kuyil method")

**Kuyil 🐦, the curious bird**, guides every game (*kuyil* is the koel bird in her name). Each game follows the same five steps:

1. **Play:** touch, drag and try things. She learns by doing first.
2. **Why? card:** a one-line answer to "why does this happen?"
3. **Imagine:** "Close your eyes… imagine you are standing on the Moon…" (read aloud, calm screen).
4. **Wow number:** one amazing fact. For example: "You are about 2,200 days old!"
5. **Talk together:** one question to discuss with a grown-up, with no right answer.

She earns ⭐ for trying and exploring, not only for right answers, so curiosity gets rewarded.

### Suggested order
Start with the ones that build on the clock she already knows: **10 Spin the Earth → 2 How Long Is a Second → 3 Time Ladder**, then **13 Planet Jump** (pure fun), and **21 Focus Garden** alongside.
Big ideas (black holes, light years, many universes) come later, but they're built on games she has already played, so they never feel out of nowhere.
