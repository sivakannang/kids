# ⚖️ Balance Scale

**Teaches:** heavier and lighter, grams and kilograms. **Age:** 5–8.

- Put fruit, a teddy, a book, a coconut, a watermelon and 100 g / 500 g / 1 kg weights on either side; the beam tilts.
- Weights are friendly round numbers ("about").
- Kuyil's 6 balancing challenges (10 bananas = 1 kg, 5 apples = 1 coconut, …).
