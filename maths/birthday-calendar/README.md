# 📅 Birthday & Calendar

**Teaches:** days of the week, months, days in each month, reading a calendar. **Age:** 5–8.

- Month calendar with today, her birthday 🎂 and special days (Republic Day, Children's Day, …). Tap a day to hear what day it is and how far away.
- Birthday countdown in sleeps, and the weekday it falls on.
- The knuckle trick for 30/31-day months.
- Kuyil's 7 calendar challenges and an 8-question quiz.
