# 🏁 Speed Race

**Teaches:** speed and time, and how huge the gap is between a snail and light. **Age:** 6–9.

- Pick up to 4 racers (snail, tortoise, walking, running, bicycle, horse, car, cheetah, Vande Bharat, plane, rocket, light) and a distance (100 m, Mysore, around the Earth, the Moon, the Sun).
- The fastest finishes in 3 seconds on screen; the others move at their true relative speed, and a results table shows the real times.
- Kuyil's 6 challenges.
