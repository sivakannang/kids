# ✋ Hands & Feet

**Teaches:** measuring with non-standard units, and why we need the centimetre. **Age:** 5–8.

- Measure a pencil, book, toy snake, door, table or bed with paper clips, her hands, her feet, a grown-up's hands, an elephant's feet or a ruler.
- A table of her measurements shows the same thing getting different answers.
- Kuyil's 6 challenges.
