# 🎂 My Life in Numbers

**Teaches:** her age counted in seconds, days, hours, heartbeats, breaths, Moon trips and km around the Sun. **Age:** 5–9.

- A grown-up enters her birthday once (saved only in this browser).
- Live age and a ticking seconds counter.
- 6 "more or less?" guess cards (a star for each right guess), revealed numbers keep ticking.
- Coming up: next birthday, next 500/1,000-day milestones, and when she turns 10,000 days old.
