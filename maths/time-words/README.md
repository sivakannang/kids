# 🗓️ Time Words

**Teaches:** yesterday, today, tomorrow, day after tomorrow, day before yesterday, last/this/next week, weekend. **Age:** 5–8.

- A 3-week calendar (last, this and next week) around today. Tap a word to light up its days, or tap a day to hear how far it is.
- Quiz with a pretend "today" so every day of the week gets practised.
- The same words in Tamil, Hindi and Kannada (with the fun fact that Hindi "kal" means both yesterday and tomorrow).
