# 🪜 Time Ladder

**Teaches:** the units of time, from a second to a millennium, and how many of each fit into the next. **Age:** 5–8.

- **Climb the Ladder:** 10 rungs (second, minute, hour, day, week, month, year, decade, century, millennium). Each rung shows "1 hour = 60 minutes" with picture dots, everyday examples, and how many seconds it holds. Reaching the top earns a star.
- **Ladder Quiz:** rounds of 8 questions mixing "Which is longer/shorter?" and "How many days in a week?". Stars for first-try answers.
- **Learn cards:** day, month and year come from the Earth, the Moon and the Sun; Thanjavur's Big Temple is over 1,000 years old.
