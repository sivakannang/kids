Time Ladder

Climb from a tiny second all the way up to a thousand years, Kavikuyil!
🐦

    🤔 Why?

    Some of time's big steps come from the sky! A day is one spin of the Earth. A month is about one trip of the Moon around the Earth. A year is one trip of the Earth around the Sun.

    Seconds, minutes, hours and weeks were made up by people, to help us share and plan our time.

    🌠 Imagine…

    Imagine a magic ladder. On the bottom step, a hummingbird flaps its wings in one second. Climb up… the Sun crosses the sky in a day. Climb higher… a mango tree grows tall over many years.

    At the very top, you can see a temple that has stood for a thousand years!

    🤩 Wow!

    One year has more than 3 crore seconds (31,536,000)!

    The Big Temple in Thanjavur was built more than 1,000 years ago. That's more than 30,000,000,000 seconds old!
