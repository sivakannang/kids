# ⏱️ How Long Is a Second?

**Teaches:** how long a second, a minute and an hour feel, and how they build up (60 → 60 → 24). **Age:** 5–8.

- **👏 Feel a Second:** a ticking stopwatch, "one-elephant, two-elephant" counting and a beating heart. Stars at 10 seconds and at 1 minute.
- **🙈 Guess the Time:** hide the clock and stop at 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds. Shows how close she got, with a bar chart of her last tries (fun to play against a grown-up).
- **👆 Tap Race:** how many taps in 10 seconds, 30 seconds or 1 minute, with a saved best score and "in one second…" facts.
- **🫙 Fill the Jars:** seconds fill the minutes jar, minutes fill the hours jar, and 24 hours make a day. Speeds from real time up to 600×.
- **Learn cards:** Why 60? (Babylon), Imagine, Wow (86,400 seconds in a day), Talk together.
