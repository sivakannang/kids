🤔 Why 60?

Long, long ago, people in Babylon loved to count in sixties. 60 can be shared fairly in lots of ways: by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20 and 30!

We still use their idea: 60 seconds make a minute and 60 minutes make an hour.

🌠 Imagine…

Close your eyes and count: one-elephant. In that tiny moment, a hummingbird flapped its wings fifty times, and a beam of sunlight zoomed around the whole Earth seven times.

One little second is full of amazing things!

🤩 Wow!

There are 86,400 seconds in one day.

If you counted one number every second, without sleeping, it would take you more than 11 days to count to one million!
