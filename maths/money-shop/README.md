# 🛒 Money Shop

**Teaches:** rupees, paying the exact amount, and working out change. **Age:** 5–8.

- Pick an item (₹2 sweet up to a ₹120 football), tap pretend coins and notes (₹1–₹100) to pay.
- Pay too much and she must work out the change.
- Kuyil's 5 shopping challenges (exact money, fewest notes, change from ₹100…).
